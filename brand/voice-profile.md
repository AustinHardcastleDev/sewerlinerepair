# SewerLineRepairList Voice Profile

## Voice summary
Practical research for homeowners who need a residential sewer lateral repaired
or replaced by a contractor whose website actually shows structural sewer work.
We separate repair contractors from treatment plants, utility offices, supply
stores, septic-pumping-only companies, and generic drain-cleaning listings so a
buyer can call someone useful.

## Core traits
- Practical filter: remove treatment plants, supply stores, utilities, and service-only dead ends.
- Sewer-literate: explain laterals, main lines, camera inspection, CIPP lining, pipe bursting, excavation, access, and restoration in plain language.
- Method-agnostic: lining, bursting, spot repair, or traditional excavation when the evidence supports it.
- Evidence-bounded: website and sewer-specific review signal, not an endorsement or license check.
- Audience-aware: homeowner copy emphasizes ownership boundary, diagnosis,
  method suitability, permits, and restoration; commercial copy emphasizes
  occupancy, shared lines, grease, access, and interruption planning.

## Preferred language
- "someone who has repaired sewer lines before"
- "sewer-repair signal"
- "residential lateral"
- "home's main sewer line" only when immediately clarified as the residential lateral
- "camera inspection"
- "trenchless or excavation"
- "confirm licensing, permits, ownership, and restoration scope"

## Avoid
- "trusted network", "vetted pros", "certified partner" without primary evidence
- implying every failed pipe can be lined
- implying every repair requires full excavation
- calling drain-snaking-only or septic-pumping-only companies sewer repair contractors
- treating a website badge as proof of current manufacturer authorization
- em dashes and AI filler
