## Decisions locked

1. Public product language defaults to **sewer line repair** / **sewer line repair contractors**.
2. Precise product is **residential sewer lateral repair or replacement**.
3. “Main sewer line” in homeowner copy always clarifies the private/building lateral.
4. Camera, hydro jetting, and root removal remain filters/support evidence, not standalone indexable proof of structural repair.
