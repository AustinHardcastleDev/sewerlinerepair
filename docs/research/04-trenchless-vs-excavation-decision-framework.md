# 04 — Trenchless vs excavation decision framework

Reviewed: 2026-08-12

## Executive summary
Trenchless methods can reduce surface disruption when pipe condition and geometry allow. They are not universal substitutes for excavation. Collapsed, severely offset, undersized, discontinuous, or otherwise unsuitable pipes may require open-cut replacement or hybrid approaches.

## Detailed findings
Decision factors: host-pipe integrity, bends/fittings, diameter change needs, depth, groundwater, adjacent utilities, cleanout access, easement constraints, and restoration cost.
CIPP and bursting each have distinct geometric and access constraints; marketing pages that claim “no digging ever” overreach.

## Claims we can make
- Trenchless can reduce surface disruption but is not a universal fit. (NASTT framing)
- Excavation remains appropriate for many collapse/offset/capacity cases. (industry practice)

## Claims to avoid
- Property-specific method prescriptions without diagnosis.
- “No dig” guarantees.

## Known gaps
- Manufacturer product limits vary; cite manuals when naming products.
