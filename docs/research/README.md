# Sewer line repair research library

Reviewed: 2026-08-12

## Purpose
Primary-source briefs that bound all public copy for SewerLineRepairList. Residential sewer lateral repair or replacement is the default product. Commercial sewer and municipal infrastructure are separately scored; municipal is internal-only at launch.

## Do not publish
- No claim that trenchless repair works for every collapsed or severely offset pipe.
- No claim that every residential sewer repair requires full excavation.
- No claim that camera inspection alone proves the condition of every section or surrounding soil.
- No generic national repair price, permit price, or timeline.
- No insurance coverage guarantee or reimbursement estimate without policy-specific primary evidence.
- No city assistance amount or deadline without primary-source refresh metadata.
- No manufacturer authorization claim from contractor website copy alone.
- No "certified trenchless contractor" claim without an official current exact match.
- No claim that root cutting repairs the defect that allowed root entry.
- No claim that lining always restores structural capacity or design diameter.
- No claim that excavation is always more expensive than trenchless.
- No blanket claim that CIPP emissions are harmless or toxic; use current method-specific occupational/public-health sources and describe uncertainty.
- No DIY excavation, sewage-handling, or confined-space instructions.
- No "permanent," "lifetime," or "future-proof" promise beyond a cited written warranty.
- No claim that FAQ JSON-LD will produce Google rich results. Google retired FAQ rich results in May 2026; keep useful FAQs visible without FAQ schema.
