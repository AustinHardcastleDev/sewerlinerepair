# 09 — Multifamily, commercial, and shared lines

Reviewed: 2026-08-12

## Executive summary
Multifamily and commercial sewer work introduces shared ownership, occupancy, access, diameter, grease, restoration, and interruption planning that single-family lateral jobs often lack. Multifamily common-line work belongs in the commercial lane for this directory. Municipal force mains, lift stations, interceptors, and large-diameter mains are municipal infrastructure, not residential laterals.

## Detailed findings
- Grease-management and interceptor work is commercial evidence, not residential ranking fuel.
- Lift-station/force-main contractors must not enter default residential collections.

## Claims we can make
- Commercial and residential evidence are scored separately.
- Municipal infrastructure evidence is internal classification at launch.

## Claims to avoid
- Ranking homeowners’ contractors using lift-station or grease-system proof.

## Known gaps
- Local shared-lateral HOA/condo rules vary.

## Primary sources
- Municipal commercial plumbing/FOG programs
- EPA collection systems materials
