# 07 — Choosing a contractor

Reviewed: 2026-08-12

## Executive summary
Directory inclusion on SewerLineRepairList means website/review-signal evidence of sewer repair work, not a license verification, endorsement, or insurance check. Buyers should still confirm jurisdiction licensing, insurance, ownership boundaries, camera diagnosis before final scope, method suitability and fallback, permits, restoration, and warranty terms.

## Detailed findings
Buyer checklist mirrors profile FAQ requirements in the playbook.
Drain-cleaning-only or septic-pumping-only firms are not structural sewer repair contractors for this directory.

## Claims we can make
- Website signal is not the same as active license verification.
- Ask for camera documentation, written method options, permit responsibility, and restoration scope.

## Claims to avoid
- “Trusted,” “vetted,” “verified contractor,” “premier.”

## Known gaps
- Official directory exact-match enrichment is optional at launch.

## Primary sources
- State plumbing/contractor boards
- NASSCO certified professionals directory (person-level credentials only)
