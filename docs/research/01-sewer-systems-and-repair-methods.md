# 01 — Sewer systems and repair methods

Reviewed: 2026-08-12

## Executive summary
A residential sewer lateral is the private pipe that carries wastewater from a building to the public sewer or septic system. Ownership of the lateral and the connection point varies by jurisdiction. Structural repair methods include traditional excavation (open cut), cured-in-place pipe (CIPP) lining, pipe bursting, and localized/spot repair. Suitability depends on pipe material, geometry, depth, access, host-pipe condition, and damage pattern. Camera inspection documents visible interior conditions; it does not replace locating, diagnosis, or permit review.

## Detailed findings
- Laterals differ from municipal mains, interceptors, force mains, and lift-station infrastructure.
- Clay, cast iron, Orangeburg, PVC, and ABS appear in different housing eras; material affects failure modes and method fit.
- CIPP inserts a resin-saturated liner cured in place; it needs a sufficiently intact host geometry and suitable defect profile.
- Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe while fracturing the old one; it needs continuous pull path and clearance for upsizing when intended.
- Excavation remains necessary for collapses, severe offsets, missing sections, capacity upgrades that trenchless cannot deliver, and many right-of-way conflicts.
- Root intrusion typically exploits existing joints/defects; cutting roots does not close the opening that allowed entry.

## Claims we can make
- Responsibility for a residential lateral and its municipal connection point varies by jurisdiction. (EPA private lateral / municipal ordinance pattern; verify locally) — verified as of 2026-08-12 against EPA wastewater/private lateral guidance framing.
- Repair methods include excavation, CIPP lining, pipe bursting, and localized repair; suitability depends on material, geometry, depth, access, and damage. (NASTT / industry practice summaries; manufacturer manuals for product-specific limits)
- Camera inspection documents visible interior conditions but is not a substitute for site-specific diagnosis. (standard municipal CCTV practice)

## Claims to avoid
- Trenchless always works; excavation always required; lining restores original diameter/capacity in every case; root cutting equals repair.

## Known gaps
- No national inventory of lateral materials by city.
- Method pricing and CIPP occupational-exposure details require method-specific current sources before numeric claims.
