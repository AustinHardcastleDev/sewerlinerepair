# 06 — Warranty, insurance, and restoration

Reviewed: 2026-08-12

## Executive summary
Written contractor warranties vary in scope, exclusions, and transferability. Homeowners insurance may exclude maintenance-related sewer failures; sewer backup endorsements and service-line coverage are policy-specific. Municipal lateral assistance programs exist in some cities and must be cited from primary pages with refresh metadata. Restoration of landscaping, driveways, and interiors should be scoped explicitly.

## Detailed findings
- Do not estimate reimbursement amounts without the reader’s policy.
- Manufacturer material warranties differ from contractor labor warranties.
- Right-of-way restoration standards are municipal.

## Claims we can make
- Readers should review their current policy, insurer, and state insurance department resources for backup/service-line coverage questions.
- Restoration scope should be written into the contract.

## Claims to avoid
- Coverage guarantees; unsourced assistance dollar amounts or deadlines.

## Known gaps
- Program amounts require dated city primary sources before publication.

## Primary sources
- State departments of insurance
- City lateral assistance pages (example pattern: Seattle side sewer materials)
- Contractor written warranty documents (deal-specific)
