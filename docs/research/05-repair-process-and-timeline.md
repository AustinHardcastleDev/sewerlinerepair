# 05 — Repair process and timeline

Reviewed: 2026-08-12

## Executive summary
A typical residential lateral project sequence: inquiry → camera inspection/locate → ownership/permit check → method selection → municipal coordination if needed → repair → inspection/testing → restoration. Timelines vary widely with permits, weather, access, and right-of-way windows. Do not publish a national “typical days” figure.

## Detailed findings
- Emergency backup mitigation may precede structural repair.
- Permit lead times dominate some municipal jurisdictions.
- Post-repair CCTV or testing may be required before sign-off.

## Claims we can make
- Buyers should confirm permit, inspection, utility-marking, and restoration responsibilities in writing before work starts.

## Claims to avoid
- Generic national timelines.

## Known gaps
- City-specific inspection checklists need primary URLs.

## Primary sources
- Municipal permit/inspection pages (jurisdiction-specific)
