# 10 — State and regional variation

Reviewed: 2026-08-12

## Executive summary
Code editions, contractor licensing, permit authority, and lateral ownership rules vary by state and often by municipality. Numeric housing fields below are intentionally zero until ACS/primary sources are attached. Zero means not populated; zeros must never appear in public copy.

## Durable facts
- Responsibility for a residential sewer lateral and its municipal connection point varies by jurisdiction.
- Permits and inspection are local even when a state adopts a statewide plumbing code.
- Work in a street, sidewalk, easement, or right-of-way may require separate municipal approval and restoration standards.
- Public utility marking and private lateral locating are different scopes.
