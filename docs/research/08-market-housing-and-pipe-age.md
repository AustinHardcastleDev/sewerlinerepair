# 08 — Market, housing, and pipe age

Reviewed: 2026-08-12

## Executive summary
Older housing stock correlates with older laterals (clay, cast iron, Orangeburg) in many U.S. metros, but material mixes are local. Census ACS provides housing tenure and year-built patterns; public-sewer versus septic shares require tables that actually support the claim. Use statewide/regional facts when city facts are undocumented.

## Detailed findings
- Median year built and owner-occupancy help explain repair demand intensity.
- Tree-root pressure, freeze depth, expansive soils, and flood exposure are regional, not universal.

## Claims we can make
- Housing age and public-sewer share vary by state and metro; cite Census tables when used in public copy.
- Older neighborhoods often need more lateral attention; do not invent pipe-material maps.

## Claims to avoid
- City pipe-material percentages without primary evidence.

## Known gaps
- Populate numeric ACS fields in brief 10 before using numbers publicly.

## Primary sources
- U.S. Census ACS: https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs
- AHS / Census sewer vs septic tables where applicable
